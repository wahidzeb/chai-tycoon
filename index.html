About Chai Tycoon

Chai Tycoon starts with one chaiwala, one kettle and a street full of thirsty customers. Tap to pour, buy more stalls, open new businesses along the bazaar, and hire managers so the whole street runs without you.

Speed milestones and upgrades stack up, events like Cricket, Eid, Monsoon and Wedding shake things up, and franchising earns Stars that make every new start faster. A Daily Chai Bonus calendar runs up to the Golden Kettle on Day 7.

3 quick tips

  1. Tap a lot in the first minute, then stop and buy. Buying is what makes you rich.
  2. Buy to milestones with NEXT, and hire the Tea Master first.
  3. Do not franchise too early. Wait until the Franchise tab offers a good handful of Stars.

How to play

  • Tap the street to serve chai. Each tap pays you and starts any shop that's waiting.
  • Buy shops: more of a shop means more profit. Owning 25, 50, 100, 200… of one shop doubles its speed.
  • Hire managers so a shop keeps running by itself, even while you're away. Offline earnings are capped (2h to start).
  • Upgrades multiply profits. Look out for events like a Cricket Match Rush or an Eid Special, and tap the golden kulhad when it floats past.
  • Franchise resets your shops in exchange for Stars. Each Star gives +2% profit forever, or you can spend Stars on permanent perks.
  • Come back every day for a bigger daily reward.

Keys: Space serve · 1–8 buy · Shift+1–8 hire · X buy amount · Tab next tab · P/Esc pause
